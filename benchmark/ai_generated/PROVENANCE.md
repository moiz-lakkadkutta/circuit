## Integrity rule

For each of the 10 prompts below, two variants were generated by answering the
prompt cold — as a general-purpose assistant would when a hobbyist asks it,
with no reference to spiceguard's checks and no knowledge of what spiceguard
would flag. **The first netlist produced for each prompt/variant is the one
saved.** Nothing here was tested against spiceguard and then edited, retried,
or cherry-picked based on whether it passed or failed. This is what makes the
silent-lie percentage in `benchmark/RESULTS.md` a defensible number rather
than a manufactured one: if spiceguard's authors could quietly discard the
inconvenient netlists, the benchmark would prove nothing.

If a netlist below has a bug (missing model, floating node, wrong device
polarity, whatever) that is exactly the kind of mistake a hobbyist would
actually paste into ngspice — it stays in the corpus as generated.
